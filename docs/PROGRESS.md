## Current status

**Phase 0 and Phase 1 are complete. Phase 2 is two hardware-blocked tasks from its gate.**

The ETL turns six government map sheets and one road geodatabase into a drivable Wan Chai in
**18.6 s across 15 stages** from a warm source cache: 66 vertex-coloured tiles at two LOD tiers, 797
road edges over 615 nodes with 217 turn restrictions, one 32k-triangle road surface with trimesh
collision, 48 fare nodes, and the published markings, tram rails, railings and traffic signs — **145
files, 56.8 MB**, which the `export` stage prints on every run. Godot streams it, the car drives it,
and it exports to a web PCK.

**Shipped in Phase 2, all five reviewed and passed:** `CityStreamer` (`P2-1`), `RoadGraph` (`P2-2`),
the queried start line (`P2-3`), the chase camera and building collision (`P2-5`), and the off-grade
carriageway on its real structure (`P2-7`). **Left:** `P2-4` (`InputRouter` across three input paths)
and `P2-6` (the perf pass) — both blocked on `P0-3b`, which needs a signing identity and the two
floor handsets. **Nothing on the critical path is blocked on software.**

**Phase 3 runs `B2` → `B1` → `B3` → `B4`** — the Hong Kong driving *experience* is finished and
tested before any taxi gameplay element exists, so test players judge the scene on its own. `B2`
carries the player taxi (`P3-11`), and `P3-9a` then puts that build in front of ≥3 HK drivers over a
web link. Order, deps and acceptance are in `PLAN.md`; the reorder and what it costs are in
`DECISIONS.md`.

**`B2` is complete and `P3-9a` is in progress — the build is re-cut, the drivers are not booked.**
Everything `B2` scoped is shipped and every task below carries its own acceptance: the taxi, the
ground, the window shader, the heroes, and — beyond the original scope — the whole published estate
of road furniture. The road draws its own markings procedurally over the lane coordinate `P1-4`
shipped, with no texture; the kerbside yellow, the tram rails, the turn arrows, the box junctions,
the railings, the traffic signs and the stop and give-way lines are all **read from what the
government publishes** rather than invented. Nothing is unstarted before the gate.

🔴 **The driver artefact is behind the bundle**, by the four landings the Bundle-size metric names.
The cut `P3-9a` goes out on is a decision rather than a detail — re-export and re-measure before
booking drivers.

⚠️ **Nothing in the repo builds the itch zip, so re-pack it by hand on every re-export.** It has
gone stale against `build/web/` once already — the packaged distributable had no road markings at
all for a day — and the mechanism is in `DECISIONS.md` `P3-9a`. The web build additionally needs
COOP/COEP headers for `SharedArrayBuffer`, which is `P3-9a`'s only open hosting item.

🟡 **`Q57`'s follow-on work is named and unassigned.** Three sweeps of the estate have now retired
"no source" claims for the markings, the carriageway width and the tramway, each one a fact measured
against Road Network v2 and then generalised — the same mechanism `Q54` and `Q56` were corrected
for. What is left to draw is listed in `Q57`.

**The premise is measured rather than assumed.** `Q8` closed when the user drove the real city: an
HK-like map is a fun enough gimmick on its own. That retires the founding risk and replaces it with a
narrower one — *gimmick* is the user's own word, and a gimmick carries a first session. "Novelty does
not survive the first session" is the live entry in the register, and it is `P3-*`'s to answer.

**Two things are knowingly missing from the world.** The **elevated network is closed to driving** —
`nearest_edge` refuses all 60 off-grade edges (`Q13`, reopened deliberately in Phase 4) — and
**almost nothing in the authenticity table is built**: no traffic, no tram *vehicles*, no neon
(`P3-3`, `P3-4`, `P3-8`) — though since `P3-14` the tram **rails** are drawn, from the source rather
than from the flag. The ground shipped with `P3-10` and is solid, so leaving the carriageway now puts the car on
the pavement instead of through it.

**Standing rules for any future cut, each learnt the hard way** (mechanisms in `DECISIONS.md`
`P3-9a` and `Q77`):

- One new name per cut, never an overwrite. Near-identical bundles are indistinguishable from the
  outside — `r3`/`r4`/`r5` sat within 960 B of each other — so **check the name, not the mtime**.
- **Re-check "0 console errors" on every cut rather than inheriting the claim** — it was true for
  `r2` and false 26 hours later (`Q77`), and `check.sh` cannot see the class.
- **Re-export before packing; never pack what a measurement left behind.** `build/web/` once held an
  ablation control that boots and renders with a layer quietly missing; its only tell was the
  console's `signs:` line.
- **Nothing in the repo builds the itch zip — re-pack it by hand on every re-export.** The web build
  additionally needs COOP/COEP headers for `SharedArrayBuffer`.
- The console's `signs:` count and `signs.json`'s **never agree**, and the offset is the check:
  `console − signs.json.triangles == text_plates × 2` — today `20,400 − 20,234 = 166`. Anything
  else is a finding, never "just the text delta".

**The premise is measured rather than assumed.** `Q8` closed when the user drove the real city: an
HK-like map is a fun enough gimmick on its own. That retires the founding risk and replaces it with a
narrower one — *gimmick* is the user's own word, and a gimmick carries a first session. "Novelty does
not survive the first session" is the live entry in the register, and it is `P3-*`'s to answer.

**Two things are knowingly missing from the world.** The **elevated network is closed to driving** —
`nearest_edge` refuses all 60 off-grade edges (`Q13`, reopened deliberately in Phase 4) — and
**almost nothing in the authenticity table is built**: no traffic, no tram *vehicles*, no neon
(`P3-3`, `P3-4`, `P3-8`) — though since `P3-14` the tram **rails** are drawn, from the source rather
than from the flag. The ground shipped with `P3-10` and is solid, so leaving the carriageway now
puts the car on the pavement instead of through it.

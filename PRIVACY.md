# Privacy

Hong Kong Taxi Ace collects no data.

- The game makes no network connection. The city is built into the app, and nothing is sent
  anywhere while you play.
- There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.
- Your settings and your best shift are saved on your own device, and are removed with the app.

The source code is public, so this can be checked: <https://github.com/williamchong/hk-taxi-Q>.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/williamchong/hk-taxi-Q/issues>.

# Privacy

Hong Kong Taxi Ace collects anonymous usage statistics, and nothing that identifies you. You can
turn them off.

- **What is sent.** When you play, the game sends a few anonymous events: a shift or free drive
  started or ended, a fare delivered or lost, and the app being installed, opened or put in the
  background. They carry numbers and settings only, such as your takings, how many fares you
  delivered or the mode you picked, and never where you drove. With them go your device's operating system, model, language and screen size, and a
  random ID made when the app is installed, which is not linked to you or to any other app.
- **What is not sent.** No name, account, email, location, contacts, photos or advertising ID.
  Events are never tied to a person, and your IP address is discarded when they arrive.
- **Where it goes.** To [PostHog](https://posthog.com), an analytics service, in the United
  States. It is used only to see how the game is played so that it can be made better. It is not
  sold, shared with advertisers or used for tracking across apps.
- **Turning it off.** On the menu, PRIVACY → SEND ANONYMOUS USAGE STATS. Off stops sending at once
  and deletes any events not yet sent.
- The game needs no connection to play. The city is built into the app, and there is no account
  and no advertising.
- Your settings and your best shift are saved on your own device, and are removed with the app.

The source code is public, so this can be checked: <https://github.com/williamchong/hk-taxi-Q>.
